# Within 1 mile of walking from an open road

Access is a walking distance (least-cost path, about 30 min), not a straight-line buffer, measured from roads
open to vehicles in the analyzed month (USFS MVUM seasons). The distance is a per-request option; the default is
1 mile. Rules out straight-line distance and year-round road assumptions.
